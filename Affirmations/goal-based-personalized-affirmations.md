# Goal-based personalized affirmations

Status: Future idea — saved for exploration, not scheduled for implementation.
Captured: November 4, 2026.
Suggested location: A section within the app's Affirmations screen; final placement to be designed.

## Eve's idea
Let people receive affirmations based on their own goals or what they want to work toward. For example, someone who wants to build wealth could receive affirmations focused on wealth and abundance.

The aim is to make affirmations more personally relevant by reflecting what the person wants in their life.

## Possible experience to explore
1. The person selects a goal category or writes their own goal.
2. Stay Positive generates a set of affirmations around that goal.
3. The person can return to those affirmations as part of their routine.

Example goal: "I want to build wealth and feel confident managing money."
Illustrative affirmation: "You build wealth through purposeful choices and growing confidence."

## Questions for a future design session
- Which categories should we offer? Possible starting points: wealth, confidence, career, relationships, wellbeing and spiritual growth.
- How should people describe a custom goal?
- Should affirmations be faith-centered by default, or should people choose their preferred style?
- Should people be able to save, edit, regenerate, share or set reminders for their affirmations?
- How many affirmations should be generated, and how often should they refresh?
- Will this be Free or Premium?
- How will goal-based affirmations relate to the existing mood-based Home recommendations?

## Next step
Develop the user flow and example content with Eve before defining implementation tasks. No technology choice, pricing decision or delivery date has been set.
