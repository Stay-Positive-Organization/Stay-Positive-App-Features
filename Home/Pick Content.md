# Pick Content by Mood (Phase 1)

The specific content behind **For your heart today**, for each mood and pick type. Use with the rules in [daily-content-module.md](daily-content-module.md#how-the-pick-is-chosen-phase-1-rules).

- **Affirmations and prayers** are original Stay Positive text, free to use.
- **Closing verses** are King James Version (public domain).
- **Meditations** are YouTube videos. This doc gives the theme and length to look for; fill in the video ID from the meditation library.
- **Journaling** has no content by design: it always opens a blank page with "Today, I…". No prompts.
- Each mood has **3 affirmations** so the 14-day no-repeat rule has something to rotate.
- Alex messages must not name the mood, because they appear on Home.

| Type | Content |
|---|---|
| Meditation | **A moment of peace** · 5 min · slow breathing, calming voice, grounding in God's presence · `videoId: TBD` |
| Prayer | **A prayer for a quiet mind** (below) · closing verse 1 Peter 5:7 |
| Affirmations | 1. I am held, even when I cannot hold it all together. 2. I can take one breath, and then the next. God is with me in both. 3. I do not have to carry tomorrow today. |
| Talk with Alex | "Hi {name}. If today feels like a lot, I'm here. We can take it slowly, one thing at a time." |

**A prayer for a quiet mind**
> Lord, my thoughts are running ahead of me.
> Slow them down, and bring me back to this moment.
> You already know what today holds,
> and You are already there.
> I give You the worries I keep carrying.
> Help me breathe, and trust, and rest in You.
> Amen.
>
> *"Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you."* — 1 Peter 5:7
