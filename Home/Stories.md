## 1. How Much Are You Worth?

> “I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made; your works are wonderful, I know that full well.”
>
> **Psalms 139:14 NIV**

When someone asks this question, our minds often rush to a monetary answer.

But a person’s true worth is measured by what money can never buy: character, peace, humility, love, and empathy.

These values are non-negotiable.

A toxic relationship destroys our sense of self. It blinds us, trapping us in a reality where we constantly try to change who we are just to fit someone else’s world. We become dependent on their validation, and life grows increasingly complicated. Nothing we do seems to be enough.

Slowly, we disconnect from ourselves.

We lose sight of the person we should love the most, our own “I.”

Everything around us begins to crumble. Family drifts away, friendships fade, and even our work suffers.

We enter a place of deep despair where the pain feels unbearable. Yet this same pain can be our doorway to healing. Pain exists to show us what is wrong. When we choose to look at our pain with compassion, we realize it has always been our greatest ally, guiding us back home to ourselves.

When we learn to value who we truly are, we become the authors of our own story.

Inner peace becomes our greatest strength, and nothing can easily shake us.

We come to know ourselves, we reconnect with God, and life begins to feel complete again.

**How much are you worth?**

It depends on the decisions you make.

The longer you stay in a toxic relationship, the longer you delay your own growth.

**How much are you worth?**

It is revealed in who you choose to become when you embrace solitude.

In that quiet space, you learn to honor your essence and show affection to the one who has never abandoned you, “yourself”.

**How much are you worth?**

It shows when you stop fighting for what no longer belongs to you.

Every human being has their own path.

Accepting that you no longer fit into someone else’s life is an act of freedom.

No one is made for everyone, and that is okay.

**How much are you worth?**

It is the measure of how strongly you believe in yourself, free from the pressures of the world.

**How much are you worth?**

It is when you choose to embrace yourself in every moment, to look in the mirror and say,

> “I love myself. No matter how the world sees me, I love myself, and I will continue to love myself.”

**How much are you worth?**

It is found in your wholehearted belief that yes, you are worthy of your own love.

**I love myself. ❤️**

## 2. Why I Believe Angels Exist

> “For he will command his angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways; they will lift you up in their hands, so that you will not strike your foot against a stone.”
>
> **Psalms 91:11–12 NIV**

I believe in angels because I have known one. Her name was Bela.

In 2006, I was going through one of the most difficult seasons of my life. My daughter was only six years old, and everything felt heavy. That was when my mother introduced me to a wonderful woman, my aunt, Bela. Very quickly, she became a second mother to me and one of the most important figures in my entire journey.

From that moment on, we talked on the phone every single week without fail.

Our bond grew stronger with each passing day. She was a constant presence, a voice of comfort, faith, and wisdom.

She was the one who connected me with a lawyer who helped guide my daughter’s court case, which began in 2009 after her father passed away. And no matter how stressful things became, Aunt Bela always repeated the same sentence with quiet certainty:

> “Claudjinha, everything has already worked out.”

Her faith was unshakable, and she passed that strength on to me.

Then came 2021, the year that broke me.

In April, my beloved aunt died from COVID. Just a few months later, in July, I lost my father to the same illness.

My world fell apart.

My finances collapsed, and I had no choice but to close the company I had built since 2016 and start again from zero.

The lowest point came on January 3, 2022.

All my bank accounts were frozen by the court. I knew I was innocent, and I fought every day to prove it. During that time, all I had left was prayer, and the more life crumbled around me, the closer I held on to God.

Then, on August 15, 2023, everything changed.

I received a call requesting that I appear in court to review some documents. When I arrived, I was given news that left me in tears:

**I won the case.**

**I was officially declared innocent.**

And the freezing of my accounts had been a grave judicial error.

After 14 long years, I was finally free from a weight I never should have carried.

But this is the part that proves angels are real.

That phone call was not just about legal victory. It was a message from heaven.

Because the date of my vindication was August 15th…

**my aunt Bela’s birthday.**

The woman who always said, “Everything has already worked out,” sent me a sign on her special day.

She was my angel on earth, and on that day, she showed me she is still my angel in heaven, cheering for me just as she always promised.

God is amazing.

I love you, my beautiful aunt and mother.

## 3. A Job for Money or a Purpose for Life? What’s the Cost?

> “There are different kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit distributes them. There are different kinds of service, but the same Lord. There are different kinds of working, but in all of them and in everyone it is the same God at work.”
>
> **1 Corinthians 12:4–6 NIV**

Let me tell you a story that carries a message many people learn too late.

Ava and Olivia worked at the same company. The conditions weren’t the best, and the salary was far from what they dreamed of.

Still, they held onto the hope that one day things would get better.

In the beginning, everything felt promising.

When Olivia arrived, she was inexperienced. Ava, who had walked the path longer, generously taught her everything she knew.

But over time, nothing changed. The company didn’t grow.

And so Olivia looked elsewhere. She found a second job, hoping it would bring the financial relief she wanted.

While Olivia worked two jobs, Ava devoted herself wholeheartedly to her one role.

She showed up with passion, curiosity, and responsibility. She learned, improved, and grew. She became skilled in many tasks and even represented the company in external meetings. Her energy was focused on becoming better, not just earning more.

Meanwhile, something else happened…

Olivia’s motivation faded.

Her energy was drained, and her work started to suffer. She spent her days complaining, longing for more money, but giving less of herself.

Her mind was fixed on what she didn’t have, instead of what she could become.

She stopped learning, she stopped growing, she stopped believing.

Then one day, life separated their paths.

Ava moved forward.

She started a new career.

Opened her own company.

Because she had invested years in learning, adapting, and giving her best, her transition was smooth and successful. And the money? It came abundantly.

Today, she lives the life she once prayed for.

This story teaches a simple truth:

**When your only fuel is money, you run out of energy quickly.**

**But when your fuel is passion, purpose, and belief in your own gift. You become unstoppable.**

Money alone can’t motivate a tired heart or inspire a discouraged mind.

Even with two jobs, Olivia ended up drained.

Ava, with one job, built a richer life. Not because she was lucky, but because she invested in her value.

Here’s the lesson:

We all carry a unique gift.

The belief that we must choose whatever pays the most, even if it kills our spirit, is outdated.

Let people flow into what they love.

Let yourself flow into what you love.

That’s where evolution happens.

Choose the path that lights your soul.

Choose the work that makes you better each day.

Choose the dream that belongs to you.

When you follow what you love, enthusiasm becomes natural.

Proactivity becomes effortless.

Growth becomes inevitable.

And money?

It always comes, and it comes in a way that reflects your worth, your passion, and the excellence you built along the way.

You can become anything you decide to be.

Just choose the path where your heart and your gift can finally breathe.

Time passes, and we need to grow.

Some continue believing that what others choose for them is what’s right.

Others decide to reshape themselves over, and over again.

These people begin to understand that life can be different, that we are all different, and that each of us is free to walk our own road toward our dreams.

Walking a different path is hard; remaining the same is also hard.

The choice is ours.

Change is painful, and great results require different actions.

The road to our destination is long.

There are days when giving up seems easier.

Our dreams must become our motivation; after all, we dream for a reason.

We can learn to be the source of our own strength.

If we choose to be different, why not continue?

Life tests us through people who disagree with our dreams, our voice, and even through those who try to discourage us from continuing.

For many, thinking big is wrong, it goes against the comfort of the majority.

But deep within those who see the world differently, there is a voice that refuses to be silenced.

Listening to that voice is what keeps them moving, even when the path is dark.

They do not wait for the light; they walk toward it.

How long will the challenge last? For them, it barely matters.

They do not chase urgent answers; they continue to defy time and turbulence.

They learned to walk by faith, not by sight.

They choose to believe that everything is possible and that, at the right moment, God will reveal the destination, one filled with blessings.

They trust that with God, everything will be much better in the end.

They do not doubt their strength; it is being shaped by God.

With every impact and every fall, they rise with greater resilience toward the place that awaits them.

They know they will arrive. No matter how difficult the path is, they will make it.

They develop the instinct that guides them.

When they fall, they feel the force that protects them.

Instead of remaining trapped in what failed, they rise and continue.

They may start over, even crawling, but they never stop. What tried to break them becomes their strength.

Every day, they grow stronger.

The battle is only against themselves.

Each day, they choose to be better than the day before.

Easy? They know it never was and never will be.

Walking one’s own path is part of life.

They understand that life has highs and lows. To stop is to signal the end.

In seasons of abundance, they are grateful.

In seasons of scarcity, they are still grateful.

Every moment becomes part of their story, and there is nothing greater than relying on God when everything seems to fall apart.

Most of the time, their journey is solitary, and they accept that.

They may cross difficult moments without anyone’s help. Not because they don’t need help, but because if help never comes, they continue walking toward their dreams alone, believing that everything is working for their highest good.

The focus is on solutions, not problems.

Every past experience is a lesson and part of the growth process.

They know helping others is divine. But in this process, they learn to be careful.

Some people appear in their lives not because they want help, but because they see their light and try to dim it.

For them, people who come to add are welcome; those who come to subtract are left behind.

Not everyone deserves to stay on the journey.

They understand how life is and have the courage to share their experiences with others without resentment.

We are all different. Each of us is unique, and that is our power.

We all carry our own dreams.

If we understood that we cannot control the choices of others, we would be happier.

We can collect advice, knowing that the decisions and consequences are entirely ours.

Along the way, we gather experiences meant to inspire, not to impose, knowing that allowing others to walk their own path is also part of our growth.

That is why growth also requires discernment.

In the end, our stories are different, our paths are different, and what worked for one may not be the answer for another, and accepting this is amazing!

Our journey shapes our destiny.

No one except ourselves can shape our destiny.

Everything we go through is part of the growth process.

Those who stay where others placed them, or walk a path designed for someone else, remain stuck, and demotivation follows them.

Some endure pain and discomfort out of fear of change, without realizing these are often signs that something must shift, because something needs to change.

Paths shape our destiny.

Every journey teaches us something. Even the moments when we are abandoned by those we never expected would leave. But if we keep walking, God will introduce us to others who share the same purpose.

This teaches us that sometime, facing fear is the only way to reach our destination.

There can never be anything good unless there is some bad along the way.

Because as long as we’re living, we must keep moving.

## 5. The Pain You Embrace Determines Who You Become

> “For our light and momentary troubles are achieving for us an eternal glory that far outweighs them all. So we fix our eyes not on what is seen, but on what is unseen, since what is seen is temporary, but what is unseen is eternal.”
>
> **2 Corinthians 4:17–18 NIV**

Life is about changing so we can become who God created us to be.

Our desires require a decision, and our dreams demand pursuit.

Life will always bring stress, challenges, and moments that test us, but no matter what, everything will be okay.

And if it’s not okay yet, it simply means it’s not the end.

Every path we walk carries either a lesson or a blessing, and for both, we must thank God.

Throughout our journey, God places people and situations in our lives according to what we desire and what we need.

Some people come and go. Some stay only for a season. And others remain for a lifetime.

During the time we spend with them, we exchange experiences, wisdom, and guidance, each of us shaping the other.

But one of life’s greatest lessons is this: we cannot force situations to become what we want.

As time passes, some people no longer serve our purpose. And when that moment comes, we must understand that it is time to let go.

Forcing relationships or circumstances only blocks our blessings.

People, just like us, make their own choices. And many are in our lives only because of what we can offer them, not because they are meant to stay.

Growing requires choices. And God always gives us signs to lead us toward our dreams.

Growing requires faith, faith to believe that even when we cannot see the path, God is still guiding our steps.

Growing requires perseverance, continuing even when it’s hard, refusing to give up on what we were called to do.

No one can walk our journey for us. We may have support, but only we can decide to follow the path toward our purpose.

When we learn to focus on ourselves, we learn how to release what isn’t meant for us.

No matter what happens, keep going. Pain will come. Hope may fade. But it is in those moments we must increase our faith in God.

> “Your capacity and your ability are not the same.
>
> Your capacity is always greater than your ability.
>
> Repeatedly using a limited ability will always expand your capacity.
>
> So, keep doing what you cannot yet do, until the day you can no longer stop doing it.
>
> And in that process, you become someone new.”

Focus on yourself. Become who you want to be. Choose wisely the people who stand beside you.

Remember: our choices shape our destiny. Surround yourself only with those who contribute to your purpose.

Never settle for less than what God has for you.

Dream boldly. Aim high. You deserve greatness, and you can achieve everything you desire.

But when you commit to growth, some people may not have the capacity to grow with you. That is why God allows certain people to leave.

The pain of growing is real. But the pain of staying the same is even greater.

When you choose to grow, the pain will eventually pass.

But when you choose not to change, the pain will deepen, and one day, regret will come.

So, ask yourself:

**Which pain belongs to you? The pain of growth, or the pain of staying the same?**

Thank You, God, for the people You placed in my life.

## 9. Trusting God in the Molt!

> “In His hand is the life of every creature and the breath of all mankind.”
>
> **Job 12:10 NIV**

Standing at Boulders Beach in Simon’s Town, South Africa, I was reminded that God speaks not only through Scripture, but also through His creation.

Penguins, gentle, resilient creatures, were moving slowly along the shore. Some looked different than others. Their feathers were falling out, their bodies appearing fragile and exposed. At first, it looked alarming. But what I was witnessing was not sickness or failure. It was purpose.

These penguins were going through what is called a catastrophic molt, a season where they lose all their feathers at once. During this time, they cannot swim. They cannot hunt. They must fast. They are forced to remain on land, completely dependent on what God has already stored within them.

Their old feathers fall away so new ones can grow.

This season looks uncomfortable. It looks messy. It looks vulnerable. Penguins often appear scruffy, almost broken, like an “exploding pillow.” Yet this process is not punishment. It is preparation.

Without this molt, they would not survive the cold waters ahead. Their old feathers can no longer protect them. God, in His wisdom, designed a season of loss so they could later walk in renewed strength.

As I watched them, God gently spoke to my heart.

We, too, go through seasons like this.

There are moments in life when things fall apart, when relationships change, when finances stretch us, when health declines, when environments become toxic, and when we feel stripped of everything familiar. These seasons can feel isolating. We may feel unable to “swim” or move forward. We are forced to pause. To fast. To wait.

But just like the penguins, this is not the end.

This is a holy process.

God allows certain things to fall away so something new can grow within us. He uses these moments to strengthen our faith, refine our character, and teach us dependence on Him alone. Sometimes we must walk through these valleys without external help, learning to trust God in silence.

It is a dark path, but not a purposeless one.

I like to call these moments “God’s tests.” Not to destroy us, but to transform us.

Pain becomes our teacher. It reveals what needs to change. It calls us to repentance, to humility, to growth. And when we choose to listen, when we choose obedience, healing follows.

If we resist change, the pain remains. But when we surrender, God renews us.

Just as penguins survive their molt, we, too, can survive our lowest seasons.

We will rise again, stronger, renewed, and prepared for the waters ahead.

Thank You, Lord, for teaching me through Your creation.

And thank you, little penguins, for reminding me that renewal often begins with surrender 🙏🙏

## 10. Control Your Response, Not the Situation

> “No discipline seems pleasant at the time, but painful. Later on, however, it produces a harvest of righteousness and peace for those who have been trained by it.”
>
> **Hebrews 12:11 NIV**

When I was watching a National Geographic documentary about magic in Brazil, one scene caught my attention and made me reflect.

In the scene, a man allowed fire to be placed on his body for a certain period of time. The agreement was that if he began to feel pain, he had to inform the team so they could put out the fire. As the fire burned, his body eventually started to feel pain, the limit of what he could endure.

This shows us that pain can be a guide. If he had not felt pain, he could have seriously damaged his body.

Pain protected him.

In the same way, this happens in many areas of our lives. Most of the time, we do not realize that God may be using discomfort to show us that something is not right.

Sometimes pain is not punishment.

Sometimes it is direction.

It is an invitation to pause, reflect, and change the course of our actions.

Taking action can lead us to a new path.

The problem is that many times we focus only on the negative, and we fail to see that it might simply be time to accept the situation and move forward.

Suffering can act as a feedback mechanism, an alarm bell telling us that something within us is out of alignment. It is a call to wake up, to stop living in a sense of powerlessness, and to take responsibility for the direction of our lives.

Discomfort can be a warning.

It can be God directing us.

Sometimes, when we ignore those warnings, God allows disappointments so that our eyes can finally open.

God may disturb our comfort so that we do not trust the wrong people or remain in places that prevent us from growing.

Next time you feel lost, ask God what He wants to teach you.

There is always a lesson.

Everything that comes into our lives is either a blessing or a lesson.

Often we focus so much on the negative that we fail to see the good that is right in front of us.

We try to control everything, forgetting that we cannot.

When our minds become trapped in constant worry and emotional ups and downs, we create chaos inside ourselves.

Discomfort can be an alert.

A signal.

A moment to pause and listen.

You cannot control what people think about you.

You cannot control most of what happens around you.

Many times, the situations that become problems grow worse because we try to solve them from a place of fear and confusion.

Instead, remember that God is always with you.

Give to Him the things that you cannot control.

Often the solution is already in front of us, but we cannot see it because our minds are filled with unnecessary worries.

Train your mind not to become a hostage to every emotion you feel.

Feeling is human.

But drowning in every feeling is a choice.

You are not every impulse.

You are the one who decides what to do with them.

## 11. People Often Project Onto You Who They Are!

> “Why do you look at the speck of sawdust in your brother’s eye and pay no attention to the plank in your own eye?”
>
> **Matthew 7:3 NIV**

Find the discernment to always be yourself, even while listening to the advice of others.

At the end of the day, the consequences of your actions will always be yours.

Be careful who you listen to.

Sometimes we are already right about a decision, but we allow outside voices to change our direction. And in the end, we don’t just face our own consequences. We carry the weight of choices influenced by others.

God is our guide. Always listen to your inner voice.

If you are in doubt, pause before making a decision and ask God what truly matters for you. He will answer, sometimes through peace, sometimes through clarity, and sometimes through the right person who understands your needs.

We all have one guide within us: our inner voice, God with us.

Not everyone walks in your shoes, and that’s okay.

Not everyone will understand your story, and that’s okay too.

So don’t let people guide your life.

The problem begins when we try to please everyone.

Sometimes, it’s better to stay silent when you don’t have the right people to talk to.

Sometimes, you don’t need advice, you just need to listen to your own heart.

Sometimes, you simply need to walk toward peace and healing.

And remember again: people often project onto you who they are.

Challenges are tests that come to shape us. Every action is a seed planted within our lives, and eventually, those seeds produce fruit. What we repeatedly choose, nurture, and practise becomes part of who we are.

When we do not pay attention to ourselves, unhealthy seeds can continue to grow. Every thought, reaction, and behaviour that we ignore gradually shapes our inner world. We cannot simply run from ourselves. Unless we identify and uproot what no longer serves us, we may spend days, months, or even years becoming someone we never wanted to be.

Life continually gives us opportunities to change what is not right within us. But before transformation can happen, we must be honest enough to recognise which parts of us need attention. This requires awareness, courage, and focus.

Rushing can do the same. When we try to move ahead of God’s timing, we may overlook the lessons that are preparing us for what comes next.

Through situations that we consider difficult, God may reveal the parts of us that need to be improved, strengthened, or healed. Yet we often move through life with so much haste, pressure, and comparison that we fail to understand how deeply our experiences are shaping us.

We become like bamboo in a storm: able to bend without breaking because our roots have grown deep and strong.

May we always choose to follow God and allow ourselves to be guided by Him.

May we learn to choose what is good, even when the journey takes time. May we trust the lessons, respect God’s timing, and remain faithful throughout the process.

In the end, God is preparing not only the blessing for us, He is also preparing us for the blessing.
