# Journal Prompt Collection

**Status:** Launch collection for review; not yet live.  
**Used by:** [Explore prompts](explore-prompts.md) and Reset Routine's prompt library ("Personalize Your Journal").  
**Size at launch:** 5 topics × 20 prompts = 100.

One shared, reviewed collection. It grows over time: new prompts and topics are added in Firestore (`journalPrompts`, `journalPromptTopics`) without an app update. Stable IDs stay fixed when wording changes, so entries and Reset lists never break.

**Faith** marks prompts with a faith framing outside the Faith topic (3 per topic, the "light touch").

Writing guidelines for additions are in [Explore prompts › Prompt collection](explore-prompts.md#prompt-collection).

## Adding prompts

1. Draft against the writing guidelines.
2. Give it the next free ID in its topic (`travel-21`); never reuse a retired ID.
3. Review for tone, safety and circumstance-neutral wording.
4. Add it here and in Firestore with `active: true`.
